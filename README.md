# Reinforcement Learning Algorithms from Scratch

This project presents from-scratch implementations and empirical comparisons of four reinforcement learning algorithms, covering both policy gradient and value-based methods.

## Sub-projects

- [REINFORCE vs VPG](https://github.com/Ironkid-lzy/REINFORCE-vs-VPG-from-scratch)  
  Comparison of policy gradient methods, focusing on advantage estimation and its impact on variance and training stability.
- [DQN vs A2C](https://github.com/Ironkid-lzy/DQN-vs-A2C-from-scratch)  
  Comparison of value-based and actor-critic methods under controlled settings.
